# Detachment-Theory
INDEPENDENT RESEARCH ON ITALIAN POLITICAL HISTORY AND ANALYSIS OF A COUNTRY IN CRISIS, THE RESEARCH IS IN ITALIAN FOR NOW, I WILL PROVIDE A TRANSLATION IN THE FUTURE
Below is the translation of the preface:

At least once in the adult life of a person with basic critical thinking, the thought has arisen that politics is a theater where actors don't know how to be on stage and barely know how to read the script. The central problem of Italian politics, mainly as we will see from the Second Republic onwards (1994-present), it is the difference between real politics and staging, the disaster is all to be found there: the discrepancy between reality and fiction, the pragmatism and the acting, what stands in the way, is then the ultimate cause of the failure of the modern state. Acting on the surface to mask inadequacy, incompetence, and ignorance, in this discrepancy, which is precisely the real reason for the national decline, since it creates a deficit that leads to a vicious circle of imperice and structural inadequacy, human nature fragments, becomes more fragile and unpredictable: some reconcile it with the mafia, some with corruption, some with pride, some with ignorance, some with abuses of all kinds, some with insecurity. To move toward the state we long for, we must go beyond appearances and fully experience politics, live it in reality, breathe in sociality, and not put up walls to mask the fragility of ignorance, not treat it like fans at the stadium, not compromise with the inner moral compass, and, even more importantly, always and forever question ourselves and never be stagnant.
